## Find your way around

- **Search** — press Ctrl+F (Cmd+F on macOS) in the chat, or the 🔍 button. The switch on the bar searches **This thread** (matches highlighted, Enter steps through them) or **All threads** (a list of results; picking one opens that thread with the matches highlighted).
- **Threads** — ☰ lists your conversations, grouped by date. Pin the ones you return to.
- **Notifications** — when a long task finishes or needs your approval while you are in another window, Codico tells you. Turn this off with the `codico.notifications` setting.
